## Change summaries
- 2026-10-07 [Claude] Engine: sky occlusion. At scene build, static structures (buildings, ruins, cliffs; not vegetation, grass or small props) are clustered into up to 16 boxes, voxelized and traced in 48 directions per cell (one sweep through the volume per direction, multithreaded, about 1 s for golden_valley's castle and 0.2 s for serene_meadow); each cell stores an ambient cube of sky visibility (32 MB at most, retraced only when the static geometry or the ground under it changes); lighting scales the ambient sky light and sky reflections of every shading model and water by it; set-0 binding 23 is now the volume array and the bindless textures moved to 24; environment.sky_occlusion {enabled, cell_m, rays, strength} — Interiors were lit by the whole sky (the castle hall glowed like the outdoors) — `dayfall-engine/src/world/SkyOcclusion.cpp`, `dayfall-engine/src/render/Renderer.cpp`, `dayfall-engine/shaders/include/lighting.glsl`, `dayfall-engine/docs/MAP_FORMAT.md`
